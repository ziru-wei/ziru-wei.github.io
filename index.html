My research focuses on enabling calm interactions as digital intelligence integrates into the physical environment. Through mediums such as XR and robotic interfaces, my recent projects explore empirical insights & technical methods that allow proactive agents to intervene and assist users without being obtrusive.

I envision future computers blending into the everyday physical environment through (1) intuitive integration and (2) calm interactivity.
