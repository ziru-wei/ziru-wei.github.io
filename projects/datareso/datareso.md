## The Challenge

Urban data can be hard to interpret without technical training. Interviews highlighted three barriers: joining data discussions, connecting numbers to everyday life, and understanding relationships between datasets.

**Design question:** How can an immersive, shared experience make city data easier to understand and discuss?

## Design Approach

Three ideas connect abstract data to a shared city model:

### Dynamic Simulation

Explore data through changing representations.

![Dynamic data simulation concept](https://static.wixstatic.com/media/6a062f_c25ba03cabc84ba8a9ff0f58eacff6a8~mv2.png/v1/fill/w_1572,h_430,al_c,q_90,usm_0.66_1.00_0.01,enc_avif,quality_auto/image_edited.png)

### Responsive Interaction

Connect data exploration with digital and physical feedback.

![Responsive interaction concept](https://static.wixstatic.com/media/6a062f_2e31c2e2d2b34510a4ae1ef3667f3e5d~mv2.png/v1/fill/w_1358,h_744,al_c,q_90,usm_0.66_1.00_0.01,enc_avif,quality_auto/image_edited.png)

## Prototype & Testing

Built with Unity MRTK2 and Arduino, the prototype uses fire-incident data from Fengdong, China (2017–2019).

![Prototype development roadmap](roadmap.png)

### Round 1: Proof of Concept

Eight participants explored the AR city model in pairs. Responsive interaction emerged as the most promising feature for further development.
